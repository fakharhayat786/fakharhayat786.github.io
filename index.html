Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we get started?

Message me on WhatsApp or book a strategy session. I audit your ad account and store first, then share a plan — before you spend anything on ads.

Which platforms do you manage?

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads and TikTok Ads, plus social media management and Shopify marketing for eCommerce stores.

What do you need from me?

Access to your ad account / Business Manager and your website or store. I set up or verify all tracking (Pixel, Conversions API, GA4) before campaigns launch.

How soon will I see results?

The first phase is always testing — finding the audiences and creatives that convert. Most accounts need a few weeks of testing data before they can be scaled profitably, and I report progress weekly so you always know where things stand.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely with eCommerce brands and service businesses globally — current campaigns include stores and lead generation in the UK and Europe.
